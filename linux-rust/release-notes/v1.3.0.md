# Kase 1.3.0

**Released:** 2026-11-08 · **Type:** minor release · **Breaking changes:** none

The live 3D case card now follows the real case more closely. The lid closing plays the moment you shut it, the card waits for the lid after you put the pods away, and the pods no longer jump between left and right.

## Fixed

- **The lid closing now plays on the case card.** Kase used to hear the lid shut 3.5 to 4 seconds late. It ignored the AirPods' Bluetooth adverts for 3 seconds after any other report, and the AirPods keep repeating reports for a few seconds after you put a pod away. If the AirPods dropped the PC before then, the card just disappeared with no close. Kase now reads the lid from the adverts as soon as two in a row agree, which is about half a second after you shut it. ([#75])
- **No more pods jumping between left and right.** Both pods send adverts, and Kase read the wrong bits for which pod is in the case. The two pods' adverts then disagreed, and the 3D pods swapped sides back and forth, up to 5 times in under a second. Kase now reads the right bits, and an advert has to agree with the one before it before a pod moves. The AirPods' own reports, which were always right, still win. ([#75])
- **A link drop as the lid shuts plays the close.** Sometimes dropping the PC is the only sign the AirPods give that the lid shut. When that happens with both pods in the case, the card now shows the lid closing and then folds, instead of vanishing. If a pod in the case said the lid was still open a moment earlier, Kase doesn't play a close, because the AirPods went to another device instead. ([#75])
- **The lid shows open as soon as the AirPods reconnect.** Pods in a closed case don't talk to the PC, so the first report on a new link with a pod in the case now opens the lid without waiting for an advert. ([#75])

## Changed

- **The card waits for the lid.** After you put both pods back in the open case, the live card now stays up for up to 15 seconds instead of 6. People usually close the lid 3 to 7 seconds later, and the close now plays on the card. Opening the case and doing nothing still folds after 6 seconds. ([#75])
- The island now logs what it decided (`island: case card for ...`, `island: link dropped as the lid shut`) to the system journal, which makes a wrong moment easier to trace.

## How it was checked

**A guided test on David's PC** (8 November, 5 rounds, 7 lid closes, 8 pod moves, recorded with `btmon`):

| Signal | Lid closes seen | Time after the lid shut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Adverts from a pod in the case | 7 of 7 | about 0.1 to 0.5 s |
| The AirPods' own lid packet | 5 of 7 | about 4 s |
| Kase 1.2.3 | 7 of 7 | 3.5 to 4.2 s, 2 of them only when the link dropped |

- In the same test, the AirPods' own reports placed every pod correctly. Kase 1.2.3 put a pod on the wrong side 14 times, all from adverts.
- With the lid open, advert status bit 4 meant "left pod in the case" and bit 2 "right pod in the case" in 287 of 287 adverts, whichever pod sent them. A pod out of the case keeps repeating the last lid it heard, so only adverts whose lid byte says "this pod is in the case" count for the lid.
- The AirPods dropped the PC 3.5 to 16 seconds after the lid shut, so a half-second lid signal leaves time for the close to play.

**Tests:** the whole test is now a replay test (`tests/fixtures/lid-test-2026-11-08.txt`). It runs both pods' adverts and every report through the same path the app uses. It checks that no pod lands on the wrong side, and that all 7 closes read as shut by the time David pressed Enter after closing. New island tests cover the 15-second wait, the close on a link drop, and no close when the lid was seen open. All 429 library tests and 93 island tests pass, and clippy and rustfmt are clean.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- The advert bits were measured on AirPods 4. Other models use the same advert format, but haven't been checked against a capture.
- If the AirPods' control channel stays silent and the adverts are your only source, a pod pulled in the first half-second after opening the lid can appear already out.
- Everything listed for [1.1.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v1.1.0) still applies.
