# Kase 1.0.3

**Released:** 2026-11-01 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

Switching between your iPhone and the PC is reliable again, and the left stem hold swaps mics again after the AirPods come back from the iPhone.

## Fixed

- **The PC no longer grabs the AirPods back while the iPhone is playing.** After the iPhone took the AirPods, Kase kept trying to reconnect, and an attempt that got through pulled them back to the PC in the middle of a video. Kase now waits while the AirPods themselves say another device is playing or on a call, and reconnects once it goes quiet. ([#61])
- **Kase sees the iPhone again when it connects while the iPhone is busy.** AirPods busy with another device can ignore Kase's whole setup, so Kase got no battery, ear or audio-source reports. It then couldn't tell the iPhone wanted to play, so it never handed the AirPods over, and the left stem hold changed noise control instead of swapping mics. Kase now sends the setup again until the AirPods answer. ([#61])

## What you should see

- **Playing on the iPhone while the PC has the AirPods:** they move to the iPhone and stay there until it stops playing.
- **Taking the AirPods out while the iPhone is playing:** the PC leaves them alone; it reconnects a moment after the iPhone goes quiet.
- **Holding the left stem:** swaps between the desk mic and the AirPods mic (with the mic switch on), not noise control.
- If the AirPods ignored the first setup, the log shows `ignored the setup; sending it again`.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.
