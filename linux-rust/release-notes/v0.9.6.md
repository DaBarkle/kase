# Kase 0.9.6

**Released:** 2026-11-01 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

Pressing play in Plex on the PC now brings the AirPods back from your iPhone, even right after they reconnected, instead of hanging with no sound.

## Fixed

- **Plex brings the AirPods back.** When the AirPods reconnect to the PC while your iPhone is using them, they don't always say who has them. Kase waited to hear, and since the Plex app doesn't tell Linux when you press play, nothing brought the AirPods back: Plex hung with no sound until you pressed On in Kase. Now any sound that starts on the PC while that's unknown takes the AirPods back, like pressing play in a browser or music player already did. ([#49])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- When your iPhone takes the AirPods first, the PC still has to wait for the iPhone to let go.
- Everything listed for [0.9.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.9.0) still applies.
