# Kase 0.11.0

**Released:** 2026-11-01 · **Type:** minor release · **Breaking changes:** none

Open the case with AirPods 4 and the island now shows it the way an iPhone does: a 3D case whose lid swings open as both pods rise out, played smoothly at your monitor's refresh rate.

## Added

- **3D case-open animation for AirPods 4.** When AirPods 4 or AirPods 4 (ANC) connect from the open case, the case card grows to show the case opening and the pods rising out, then holds the open case above your battery rings. It is rendered from a detailed AirPods 4 model at 144 frames a second and timed by the clock rather than by frames, so it stays smooth on 60, 120 and 144 Hz screens alike. The **AirPods** tab plays it above your AirPods' name. ([#51])
- **Light on resources.** Frames load only when the animation shows, a few at a time on a background thread, and are freed five seconds after it goes. On a 144 Hz desktop it uses about 11 MB of extra memory for the 1.6 seconds it plays.

## Changed

- With **Reduce motion** on, the case card and the AirPods tab show the open case without the animation. Other AirPods models keep the case card exactly as before, and the case card is still never shown over a fullscreen game. ([#51])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- The animation is for AirPods 4 only. Other models show the case card without it.
- Everything listed for [0.10.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.10.0) still applies.
