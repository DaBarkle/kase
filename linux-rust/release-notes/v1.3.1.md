# Kase 1.3.1

**Released:** 2026-11-08 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

The live 3D case card works again when the AirPods connect but say nothing on their control channel. The card shows the case open, each pod coming out and going back in, and the lid closing.

## Fixed

- **The case card no longer goes blank when the AirPods' control channel is silent.** Now and then the AirPods connect and play audio, but never answer on their control channel. Then the Bluetooth adverts are Kase's only source for the lid and the pods. Kase 1.3.0 moved the lid or a pod only after two adverts in a row agreed. But the Linux Bluetooth service (BlueZ) only passes an advert on when its content changes, so the second matching advert often never came. The card then had no case to draw: no lid opening, no pods, and no close. Now an advert that arrives on its own after a quiet spell (0.6 s or more) counts straight away. A quick burst of adverts that disagree, from the two pods while the lid moves, still needs two in a row. ([#76])

## How it was checked

**On David's PC (8 November, 09:55 Melbourne time):** the AirPods connected and audio started, but the control channel never answered Kase's setup (`ignored the setup; sending it again`). Kase got adverts as the lid opened, the pods came out and the pods went back in, but its case tracker logged no change at all. The island showed, with no 3D case.

**Tests:**
- The 8 November lid test replays a second time with only the adverts BlueZ would pass on (a pod's advert only when it changed). It puts no pod on the wrong side and catches all 7 closes on time, the same as the full replay.
- A new test plays a silent control channel with adverts seconds apart: the lid opens, both pods come out and go back, and the lid closes.
- Another checks that a burst of disagreeing adverts still needs two in a row.
- All 432 library tests and 93 island tests pass, and clippy and rustfmt are clean.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- When the control channel is silent, the AirPods still drop the PC when you shut the lid, and Kase hears the close from the adverts or from that link drop.
- Everything listed for [1.3.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v1.3.0) still applies.
