# Kase 1.0.2

**Released:** 2026-11-01 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

The AirPods 4 3D animations are back. Kase 1.0.1 shipped the fixed animations but didn't show them.

## Fixed

- **The 3D animations show again.** Kase looked for the case-open animation by its exact number of frames. The fixed animation in 1.0.1 is a little shorter, so Kase decided no 3D art was installed and showed none, on the island's case card or in the AirPods tab. It now finds every animation by its first frame and counts the frames itself, and a new test checks that every animation is found. ([#60])

## What you should see

- **Opening the case at your desk:** the island's case card plays the case opening and the pods lifting out. The first time in six hours, the closed case spins round first and the pods swirl out.
- **Putting both pods back:** the case card shows them dropping in and the lid closing.
- **The AirPods tab:** the case open with the pods out while connected, closed while they're put away or out of range.
- The case card never shows over a fullscreen game or video, and with **Reduce motion** on you see just the final frame.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- Everything listed for [1.0.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v1.0.0) still applies.
