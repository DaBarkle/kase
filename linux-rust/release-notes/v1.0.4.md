# Kase 1.0.4

**Released:** 2026-11-01 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

Holding the left stem swaps mics again while your iPhone is connected alongside the PC.

## Fixed

- **The left stem hold swaps mics again.** The AirPods send stem holds to whichever device they mark as active. With your iPhone connected alongside, that mark could stay on the iPhone even while the PC was playing, so the AirPods handled the hold themselves and changed noise mode. Kase now notices this and claims the AirPods, so the mark moves to the PC (at most once every 30 seconds, so it never fights the iPhone). ([#62])

## What you should see

- **Holding the left stem at the PC:** swaps between the desk mic and the AirPods mic (with the mic switch on), even with the iPhone connected.
- The log shows `Another device is marked active while this PC plays; claiming the AirPods` when Kase moved the mark.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations
