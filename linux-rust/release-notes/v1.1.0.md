# Kase 1.1.0

**Released:** 2026-11-02 · **Type:** minor release · **Breaking changes:** none

With AirPods 4, the 3D case on the case card now follows your real case: open the lid and it opens, take a pod out and that pod lifts out, put it back and it drops in.

## New

- **The case follows you live.** The case card used to play one fixed clip when the lid opened. Now it shows what you're actually doing, as you do it ([#64]):
  - The lid opens when you open it. On the first open of the day, the case turns a full circle first.
  - Each AirPod lifts straight out of its well when you take it, turns once and hovers beside the case. Take the right one first and the right one rises; the left one waits until you take it.
  - Put a pod back and it drops into its well.
  - Close the lid and it closes. If a pod is still in your ear, that pod keeps hovering beside the case.
  - Change your mind halfway (pull a pod out and put it straight back) and the animation plays back from where it is. It never jumps.
- **Follow the case live** is a new setting in the Island tab. It is on by default. Turn it off to get the earlier fixed clips.
- **`tools/case-watch.py`** shows what Kase sees of the case, live, for anyone testing by hand.

## Changed

- **The case card stays for the whole moment.** It comes back for each change while the case is in play and folds away 6 seconds after the last one, or 1.5 seconds after the lid shuts. Before, it folded 2 seconds after the first pod came out, which would cut the new animation short.

## Fixed

- The island didn't notice a second pod coming out while the first was already out. Each change to the case now counts.
- **The status pill in the app window no longer flickers.** After each change, its text (such as "Connected · Adaptive") blinked on and off for about a second while the pill's width settled. The text now keeps its full width and the pill only clips it. ([#65])

## Documentation

- The README now opens with 60 fps demos of the real app: the island opening into its card, its alerts, and the app window connecting. ([#65])

## How reliable it is

Kase works out where each pod is from two sources:

- The AirPods' own in-ear and in-case reports, which arrive within about a tenth of a second.
- The Bluetooth adverts, which also report the lid. Kase uses these in the first second after the lid opens, before the AirPods start talking to the PC.

Replaying every case event the AirPods sent on 1 November (2,779 pod readings across 67 connections), Kase put every pod in the right place. The animation was also tested against 3,000 random streams of late, missing, repeated and out-of-order reports, and always arrived at the right picture without a jump.

## What you should see

- **Lid open, both pods in:** the lid opens with both pods seated.
- **Right pod out, then left:** the right one rises and hovers, then the left one.
- **Both back in, lid closed:** each one drops in, the lid closes, and the card folds 1.5 seconds later.
- Kase's log shows a line such as `case … #12 at 01234 ms: lid Open, L Seated, R Out (Aacp)` for each change.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- The live case is drawn for AirPods 4 only. Other models keep the earlier card.
- If the AirPods send no reports at all while connected (it happens on some connections), Kase falls back to the adverts, which can lag a real change by about a second.
- Everything listed for [1.0.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v1.0.0) still applies.
