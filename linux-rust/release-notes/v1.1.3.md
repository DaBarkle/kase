# Kase 1.1.3

**Released:** 2026-11-02 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

Holding the left stem swaps between your desk mic and the AirPods mic reliably again, instead of sometimes changing noise control.

## Fixed

- **The left-stem hold swaps mics every time.** Every so often, the AirPods open their control channel to the PC and then never send anything on it. Sound plays as normal, so they look connected, but the battery, ear detection and Kase's claim on the stem hold never get through. So holding the left stem changed noise control, as it does when Kase isn't there. Kase's logs show this on 13 of 75 connections since 29 October, mostly when the case had just been opened with the pods still inside. It isn't something 1.1.0 to 1.1.2 broke. Kase used to send the setup again on the same channel, but that never got an answer. Now, if the AirPods have said nothing for 12 seconds, Kase closes the channel and opens a fresh one. It keeps doing this for as long as the AirPods are connected, waiting 1, 2, 5, 10 and then at most 30 seconds between tries. Your music keeps playing while it does this. ([#68])

## How it was checked

- Kase's own log from David's PC on the morning of 2 November was replayed. That connection stayed silent through five setup resends, and the left hold changed noise control. New tests cover the timing: a channel that talks is never closed, a quiet one gets the setup once more at 4 seconds, a silent one is replaced at 12 seconds (before the 20-second stuck-session check), and the retries go on with waits capped at 30 seconds. Another test checks that any packet at all, even one Kase can't read, counts as the AirPods talking.
- All library tests pass, and clippy and rustfmt are clean.
- `docs/TESTING.md` has a new step for this (section 7, step 5) and a new row in the case and reconnect table.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- If a fresh channel doesn't bring the AirPods back either, the log keeps showing `Opening a new control channel` lines. The next step would be a full Bluetooth reconnect, which Kase doesn't do on its own yet because it would cut your sound for a moment.
- Everything listed for [1.1.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v1.1.0) still applies.
