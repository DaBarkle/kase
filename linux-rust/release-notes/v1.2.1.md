# Kase 1.2.1

**Released:** 2026-11-06 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

Your AirPods connect by themselves after a connection drops halfway, instead of staying off the PC until you restart the Bluetooth service.

## Fixed

- **AirPods no longer stay off the PC after a connection drops halfway.** When you open the case, Kase connects at once. If the AirPods drop that connection within a second or two, which can happen when an iPhone is nearby, BlueZ tells Kase the connect was cancelled but keeps its list of unfinished work. From then on it refuses every connect as busy, from Kase and from any other app. The case card showed for a moment and went away, and opening the lid or taking the pods out did nothing, because the card only shows while connected. Only restarting the Bluetooth service got them back. Kase now recognises this and clears it with an ordinary Disconnect, which needs no root and does nothing else while no link is up, then connects again. It acts straight away when a cancelled connect is followed by a busy answer, and otherwise after 15 seconds of busy answers, which is longer than a real connect attempt takes. ([#70])

## How it was checked

- Kase's log and the Bluetooth service log from David's PC on the evening of 5 November show the failure: a connect cancelled at 13:11:40 UTC, 20 busy refusals over the next three and a half minutes, then a connection two seconds after the Bluetooth service was restarted.
- The BlueZ 5.87 source confirms the cause. When a link drops it answers the waiting connect but doesn't empty the list that causes the busy refusal, and Disconnect does empty it.
- New tests cover when Kase decides BlueZ is stuck, how it reads BlueZ's errors, and that its own Disconnect is never mistaken for you pressing Disconnect, which would stop reconnects for good.
- All library tests pass, and clippy and rustfmt are clean.
- **Not yet seen working on real AirPods.** This failure can't be triggered on demand, so the clearing step hasn't run against a stuck BlueZ yet. When it does, the log says `BlueZ is stuck on an old connect`. `docs/TESTING.md` has a new row for this in the case and reconnect table.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- If the Disconnect doesn't clear BlueZ, Kase tries again every time the busy answers have gone on for 15 seconds, and `sudo systemctl restart bluetooth` is still the fallback.
- Everything listed for [1.1.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v1.1.0) still applies.
