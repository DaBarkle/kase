# Kase 1.2.2

**Released:** 2026-11-06 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

Videos on your iPhone keep playing when you're at the edge of the PC's Bluetooth range, and when you scroll a feed of short videos. Kase no longer pulls the AirPods back to the PC in those moments.

## Fixed

- **A reconnect no longer pulls the AirPods off your iPhone.** When the link to the PC dropped, Kase kept remembering that the PC had the audio. At the edge of range the link drops and comes back often, and every time it came back Kase claimed the AirPods within a second. It didn't check whether the iPhone was playing a video or even on a call. Kase now forgets who had the audio when the link drops and waits for the AirPods to say who's playing. It only makes that claim while the PC is really playing and the iPhone is neither playing nor on a call. ([#72])
- **Short videos on the iPhone are no longer cut off.** Instagram and Facebook reels tell the AirPods "stopped" between clips. When the next "playing" came a few seconds late, Kase decided the iPhone was done, took the AirPods back after 5 seconds, and the video on the iPhone paused. Once the iPhone has played a video, Kase now waits for 45 seconds of quiet before bringing the AirPods home. If you move the AirPods back to the iPhone after Kase took them, it waits 2 minutes. ([#72])

## Changed

- **No automatic take-backs while the link to the PC is unsteady.** For 3 minutes after the AirPods drop off the PC while you're wearing them, Kase leaves them on the iPhone. Pressing play on the PC, or **Use on this PC**, still brings them back straight away. ([#72])

## How it was checked

**What the logs show:**

- On 5 November, between 13:25 and 13:58 UTC, Kase took the AirPods back 9 times while reels played on the iPhone. Every one came 2 to 5 seconds after the iPhone's "stopped" message between clips. Twice the iPhone took them straight back.
- Since 29 October, Kase claimed the AirPods within a second of reconnecting 5 times. On 2 November at 03:02 UTC the iPhone was on a call when that happened.

**Tests:** new tests replay the reel pattern, the move-back case, the unsteady link, and check that a dropped link forgets who had the audio. All library tests pass, and clippy and rustfmt are clean. `docs/TESTING.md` has new rows in the iPhone switching table.

**Not seen on real AirPods yet:** the new timings come from the logs. Watch for `the link to this PC just dropped` and for a longer `quiet for` value in the `taking the AirPods back` log lines.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- After you stop a video on the iPhone, the AirPods now take about 45 seconds to come home instead of about 5. Pressing play on the PC brings them back at once.
- Everything listed for [1.1.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v1.1.0) still applies.
