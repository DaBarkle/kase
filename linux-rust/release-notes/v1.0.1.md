# Kase 1.0.1

**Released:** 2026-11-01 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

The AirPods 4 animations no longer pass through the case. The pods now lift straight out of their wells before they spin, sit side by side in the case instead of overlapping, and the lid opens and closes clear of them.

## Fixed

- **The pods no longer pass through the case on the first connect of the day.** In 1.0.0 the pods started spiralling while they were still in the case, so they swung through its walls and the open lid. Now they lift straight up out of their wells first, and only spin once they're clear, drifting a little apart and upwards so they never touch each other or the lid. ([#59])
- **The pods sit properly in the case.** In the open case the two pods overlapped each other in the middle, and the lid brushed through them as it opened. They now sit side by side, each in its own well, facing forward, in all three animations (case open, spin and open, and put away). ([#59])
- **Putting the pods away matches.** The pods turn to their seated angle above the wells, drop straight in, and then the lid closes. ([#59])

## Under the hood

- A new collision check (`tools/render3d/collide.py`) tests every frame of every animation for parts passing through each other, and the seated pose was solved with it. All three clips pass with no collisions at any frame. ([#59])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- Everything listed for [1.0.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v1.0.0) still applies.
