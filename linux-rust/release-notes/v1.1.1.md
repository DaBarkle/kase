# Kase 1.1.1

**Released:** 2026-11-02 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

The live 3D case now always moves the AirPod you actually took out or put back.

## Fixed

- **The right pod no longer shows as the left one.** The AirPods don't say "left" and "right" when a pod goes in or out of the case. They say "first pod" and "second pod", and which one is first changes as you use them. Kase works out which is which from the order of their battery report. It was reading that order from its saved battery list, which could still hold the order of an older report. So after the AirPods swapped which pod comes first, putting the right pod back could show the left one dropping in. Kase now uses the order of the newest report. ([#66])

## How it was checked

- David's test on the evening of 1 November is replayed as two new tests. One covers the right pod going back in after both had been worn. The other covers the right pod coming out while the AirPods swapped which pod is first. Both failed before the fix and pass now.
- All 400 library tests and 89 island tests pass.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.
