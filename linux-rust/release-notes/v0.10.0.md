# Kase 0.10.0

**Released:** 2026-11-01 · **Type:** minor release · **Breaking changes:** none

Kase now moves the way the island always has. Battery numbers roll like an odometer, rings fill one after another when the AirPods connect, and charging rings come alive. Tab pages slide, and a click on the tray icon opens a quick look with your battery and noise control. If you'd rather have less motion, the new Reduce motion setting follows your desktop's own animation setting.

## Added

- **Quick look from the tray.** Left-click the tray icon and a small black card springs out of the corner by the tray. It shows the left, right and case rings, a row to change noise control, and **Open Kase** for the full window. It goes away when you move off it, press Esc or click the icon again. On desktops where it can't be shown (GNOME), or if you switch off **Quick look from the tray** in **Settings**, the click opens the window as before. Right and middle click are unchanged. ([#50])
- **Reduce motion.** A new setting in **Settings**: System, On or Off. On System it follows your desktop: KDE's animation speed set to *Instant*, or GNOME's *Animations* switched off. With it on, slides and shape changes become short fades and nothing loops, in the window and on the island alike. ([#50])
- **Keyboard tab switching.** Ctrl+Tab and Ctrl+Shift+Tab move between the tabs, with a focus ring on the tab bar. ([#50])

## Changed

- **Numbers that roll.** In the window, battery percentages roll like an odometer when they change. When the AirPods connect or the window opens, the left, right and case rings fill one after another and the numbers count up with them. ([#50])
- **Charging that looks alive.** When charging starts, the bolt pops in with a little bounce and one green ripple leaves the ring. A faint light then runs round the ring while it charges. On the island this light runs a few times and never over a fullscreen game. ([#50])
- **Pages that slide.** Switching tabs slides the page in from the side of the tab you picked. The status pill at the top stretches to fit its new text, and its words and glow colour cross-fade instead of snapping. Buttons dip slightly while you press them. ([#50])
- **A livelier island.** When a card opens, its rings fill in turn, and content comes in with a soft focus pull. The L or R label dims while that pod is out of your ear. ([#50])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- The quick look opens in the screen corner nearest the tray, guessed from where your panel says you clicked. With a tray in the middle of a top or bottom panel it opens in the right-hand corner.
- If the island process is still starting (right after login) or restarting, a tray click opens the window instead of the quick look.
- iced buttons can't be focused with the keyboard yet, so keyboard focus covers the tabs and text fields only.
- Everything listed for [0.9.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.9.0) still applies.
