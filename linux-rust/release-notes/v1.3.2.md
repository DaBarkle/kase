# Kase 1.3.2

**Released:** 2026-11-08 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

When you put both AirPods in your ears and shut the case, the live 3D case card now shows the lid closing before it folds away.

## Added

- **The lid closes on the card with both pods in your ears.** Only a pod sitting in the case knows whether the lid is open. Once both pods are in your ears, the AirPods lose touch with the case: they report it as disconnected, and none of their Bluetooth messages says the lid shut. Kase 1.3.1 then folded the card with the lid still open. By that point you've almost always closed the case, so now, when the card folds with both pods in your ears, it first plays the lid closing over the empty case. The card says "Case closed" and folds 1.5 seconds later. This uses a new 3D clip of the empty case closing. If a pod is in your hand rather than your ear, the case may well still be open, so the card just folds as before. ([#77])

## Fixed

- **The case card folds on time after a close played on the way out.** When the AirPods drop the PC as the lid shuts, the card plays the close and should fold 1.5 seconds later. Nothing woke the island at that moment, so the card could stay up until the next change. ([#77])

## How it was checked

- **On David's PC (8 November):** with 1.3.1, both pods went straight into his ears, and closing the case played no close. The card just folded.
- **On screen:** a recorded island journey (`tools/visual-qa/journeys/j12-both-in-ears-close.json`) shows the card plays the empty-case close, says "Case closed", and folds 1.5 seconds later.
- **3D art:** the new clip `lid-close-none` (48 frames at 144 fps) passed the collision check with both pods hovering beside the case.
- **Tests:** a new island test covers the close with both pods in your ears, and that a pod in your hand only folds the card. All 432 library tests and 94 island tests pass, and clippy and rustfmt are clean.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- The close with both pods in your ears is a best guess, because the AirPods can't tell. If you leave the case open, the card still shows it closing.
- Everything listed for [1.3.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v1.3.0) still applies.
