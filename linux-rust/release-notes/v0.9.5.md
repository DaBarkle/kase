# Kase 0.9.5

**Released:** 2026-11-01 · **Type:** patch release · **Breaking changes:** none

When a call or ring on your iPhone ends, the AirPods now come back to the PC by themselves, instead of leaving your game and Discord silent until you press On in Kase.

## Fixed

- **The AirPods come back after a call or ring on the iPhone.** Your iPhone tells the AirPods when a call starts, but often never says when it ended. Kase waited for a call that was already over, so game, Discord and other PC audio stayed silent until you pressed On. Kase now also listens for the iPhone saying it is idle again, takes that as the end of the call, and brings the AirPods back a few seconds later. During a real call they still stay on the phone. ([#48])

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- When your iPhone takes the AirPods first, the PC still has to wait for the iPhone to let go.
- Everything listed for [0.9.0](https://github.com/DaBarkle/kase/releases/tag/v0.9.0) still applies.
