# Kase 1.0.0

**Released:** 2026-11-01 · **Type:** major release · **Breaking changes:** none

Kase 1.0 is the release where AirPods on Linux feel finished. The island now tells one calm story for each thing you do, the case greets you with a new 3D animation on the first connect of the day, the whole app shares one Apple-grade look, and a week of reliability work on audio, sleep and switching is in. Everything since 0.1 is summed up at the end.

## Highlights

- **A calmer island.** Put your AirPods away one pod at a time and the island shows one alert and one case card, not a flurry of messages that replace each other. Kase waits a moment for the AirPods to finish reporting, joins alerts of the same kind, updates an open card in place and never swaps a message before you've had time to read it.
- **The case spins on your first connect of the day.** Open the case at your desk in the morning and the 3D case turns once, opens, and both pods spiral up out of it. Putting both pods back plays them dropping in and the lid closing.
- **One look everywhere.** The island and the window share Apple's current system colours and spring limits, the AirPods tab keeps the same shape for every model and fits without scrolling, and the light theme is polished throughout.
- **Steadier audio.** The AirPods mic switch, sleep and resume, and switching back from your iPhone all got sturdier.

## Added

- **Spin and open.** On the first connect of the day (more than six hours after the last spin), AirPods 4 play a new animation in the case card: the closed case turns 360 degrees, the lid opens and the pods spiral out, settling with a small bounce. Later connects play the familiar case-open clip. ([#57])
- **Put away.** Putting both pods back into the open case shows the case card for three seconds with the pods dropping in and the lid closing. ([#57])
- **The case going on a charger is announced** ("Case charging") while the pods sit in the open case.
- **Design rules and journeys.** `docs/DESIGN.md` sets the rules every screen follows: Apple's Live Activities guidance as the base, plus Kase's own 3D layer with rules for where and when it plays. `docs/JOURNEYS.md` describes what the island should show in ten everyday moments, from opening the case at the desk to a low battery mid-game.
- **Frame-exact visual tests.** `just visual-journeys` plays those ten journeys on a test clock and records every frame of every animation into filmstrips, real-speed videos and automatic checks (a message replaced too soon, the island cut off, motion that runs too long). A nightly workflow keeps the recordings fresh. ([#55])

## Changed

- **One thing you do, one message.** Kase gathers each burst of AirPods updates for 0.3 seconds (at most 1.5) before reacting, which covers the 46 to 113 ms the AirPods take to follow an ear change with a battery update. Repeats and small level changes still show at once, they just don't start a new message. ([#57])
- **Alerts join up.** Left then right charging becomes "AirPods charging"; quick noise control changes become one alert showing where you ended up.
- **An open card updates in place.** News appears on its second line and the card stays up three seconds after the last change. A new message waits until the current one has been on screen for 1.2 seconds.
- **The case reading coming and going is not news.** The AirPods report the case as unknown for a couple of seconds whenever a pod goes in, and as disconnected while both pods are out; that no longer pops the card.
- **Low battery warns once.** One warning at 20% or less and one at 10% or less per discharge. A level wobbling between 20 and 21 no longer repeats it; charging or rising five points re-arms it.
- **The art tells the truth.** Put away or out of range, the AirPods tab shows the case closed rather than open. On the island, the case animation starts once the card has finished opening, so its best part is no longer hidden behind the morph.
- **One palette and one set of springs.** The island and the window now use the same Apple system colours (the two slightly different greens are gone) and the same named springs, with no bounce above Apple's 0.4 limit. The charging light runs one pass round the ring, inside Apple's two-second guideline.
- **The AirPods tab keeps its shape.** AirPods 4 get a 200 px hero for the 3D case; other models get their glyph in a slot of their own, so the page never jumps. The window is 700 px tall by default, so the AirPods tab fits without scrolling in every state.
- **Light theme.** The status pill is white with a hairline edge instead of a black capsule, switch and slider knobs have an edge, and the Island tab preview keeps its icon.
- **Smaller touches.** Even padding on both sides of the island card; one case icon on the slim island; pointing at a noise control mode in the tray quick look names it; the case's "last seen" caption fits on one line; **Rename** lights up once you've typed a new name.
- **Switching back from the iPhone listens to the AirPods too.** Their Bluetooth adverts say whether the device that has them is idle, playing or on a call. Kase never takes them back during a call, waits while they say the iPhone still plays, and brings them home when they say nothing plays. ([#54])
- **A lighter fix for a silent right pod.** After the AirPods mic stops, Kase restarts only the sound output instead of switching the AirPods back and forth. ([#56])
- **Audio profiles are set without being saved,** so WirePlumber never remembers a temporary profile as your default. ([#56])
- **Tidy before sleep.** Kase finishes its audio work before the PC suspends and waits a little longer for BlueZ to confirm a profile. ([#56])

## Fixed

- **A Disconnect pressed just before quitting is remembered.** Quitting within three seconds of a Disconnect used to lose it, and the AirPods reconnected on the next start. ([#53])

## Security

- Updated `crossbeam-epoch`, `quick-xml` and `wayland-scanner` for RUSTSEC-2026-0204, -0194 and -0195 (`quick-xml` is only used while building). ([#54])

## Kase so far

Kase began as a fork of LibrePods for Bazzite and became its own app on the way to 1.0:

- **Battery you can trust:** left, right and case levels read over Apple's own control channel, with the case's last known level kept when it goes quiet.
- **The island:** a Dynamic Island over your desktop and fullscreen games that never touches the game, draws nothing while it rests, and comes in Purist and Classic styles and four sizes.
- **The window:** AirPods, Island and Settings tabs, a tray icon with a quick look card, light and dark themes, and Reduce motion.
- **Your AirPods' own controls:** noise control, conversation awareness, stem press track control, renaming and the AirPods mic switch.
- **Switching like a Mac:** Apple device mode, taking the audio back from your iPhone when it stops playing, instant connect when you open the case, and reconnecting by itself after the AirPods wander out of range.
- **Runs widely:** KDE Plasma 6 gets every feature; other desktops get the window and tray; x86_64 and ARM64 Flatpak bundles; one-line install and update.

## Upgrading

Open a terminal and run:

```bash
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/DaBarkle/kase/main/linux-rust/packaging/install.sh | bash
```

Kase restarts in the tray with your settings, saved AirPods and Flatpak overrides intact.

## Known limitations

- The 3D animations are for AirPods 4. AirPods Pro and Max show their glyph until free, properly licensed models of them exist.
- When an iPhone is nearby and awake, it may take the AirPods first when you open the case; Kase takes the audio back once the iPhone stops playing.
- The ARM64 bundle is built and tested in CI but hasn't been run on ARM hardware yet.
- The GNOME island extension is not part of 1.0; on GNOME, Kase runs as a window and tray app.
